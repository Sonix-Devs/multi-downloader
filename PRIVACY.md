# Privacy Policy — Multi Downloader

_Last updated: 2026_

## What we process

Multi Downloader processes the URLs you paste in order to fetch public
metadata (title, thumbnail, duration, available formats) via `yt-dlp` and to
download the corresponding media file to your chosen folder.

We store a local history of your downloads (URL, title, thumbnail, status,
file path) in the app's database so you can find files again later. This
history stays on the machine running the app and is never transmitted to a
third-party analytics service.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell, rent, or share your download history or clipboard contents
  with anyone.
- We do not require an account, email address, or payment information to use
  the app.
- Clipboard access is read-only, opt-in via Settings, and only used to
  suggest pasting a link you already copied — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Third-party requests

To fetch metadata and media, the app's backend (`yt-dlp`) contacts the
platform the link belongs to (e.g. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram). Those
platforms may log the request per their own privacy policies — Multi
Downloader does not control or influence that behavior.

## Data retention

You can clear your download history at any time from **Settings → Danger
zone → Clear all history**. This removes the metadata records; it does not
delete files already saved to disk unless you explicitly choose to.
